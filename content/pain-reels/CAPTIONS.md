# Pain-point posts: captions to go with the Reels

One block per problem from the seller survey (`OWNER_PAINS`, lib/owner-survey.ts). The Reels are
rendered with `npm run pain-reels:render` into `demo-output/pain-reels/<pain>.mp4`; use
`<pain>.cover.jpg` as the cover. Hooks are Hinglish, the rest English, the way sellers write on
Instagram.

Rules for anything posted from here:

- Say only what the product does today. The trial is 30 days, no card (`TRIAL_DAYS`,
  `TRIAL_THEN_NOTE`); prices come from `lib/plan-copy.ts`, so don't type a price into a post.
- "No commission" is about UPI: buyers pay the seller straight. Don't say "free forever".
- Four problems have no Reel yet (no footage, or no feature): text posts or nothing, as marked.

Hashtags for every post (pick 5–8): #homekitchen #homebaker #cloudkitchen #homechef #instagramseller
#smallbusinessindia #preorder #upi #homemadefood #supportlocal

## No Reel yet

### Finding new customers (`new_customers`) — text post only

The product doesn't find customers for anyone; it makes the store easy to share. Post it that way:

> Naye customers kaise laayein? Make it one tap to order. One store link in your bio, a link for each
> dish ("Copy link for Instagram"), your WhatsApp group on your store, and buyers can add your store to
> their phone's home screen like an app.

To make a Reel, record scene A6 (Share to Instagram) from docs/DEMO_VIDEOS_PLAN.md.

### Making posts and stories (`posting`) — text post only

> Har pre-order ke liye post banana? Your pre-order comes with an Instagram story image and a ready
> message for WhatsApp. Tap, share, done.

The `overselling` Reel ends on "Copy the message and post it", so it can stand in. A proper Reel needs a
story-image scene.

### Tracking stock, ingredients and costs (`ingredients`) — text post only

Stock is per product, not per ingredient: say "stock", never "ingredients".

> Kitna stock bacha hai? Set a count per product. Buyers see "Only 2 left" and then "Sold out", and
> online orders never sell what you don't have. Add what you paid and keep your latest cost on each item.

Needs scenes E2 (stock) and E3 (received stock and cost) for a Reel; better for goods stores (jewellery,
clothing) than kitchens.

### Knowing my sales and profit (`profit`) — don't post

There is no profit report yet (docs/COST_AND_PROFIT_PLAN.md, step 3). Daily sales totals exist, but a
"know your profit" post would promise something we don't have. Revisit when the report ships.
